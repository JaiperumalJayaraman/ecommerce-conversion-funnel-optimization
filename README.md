# E-Commerce Conversion Funnel & Product Optimization

An end-to-end Business Analytics and Product Management portfolio project analyzing an e-commerce customer journey, identifying conversion drop-offs, comparing performance across segments, and translating findings into prioritized product opportunities.

> **Data note:** This project uses synthetic data generated for portfolio/learning purposes. No real company performance improvement is claimed.

## Business Problem
Users can drop out between product discovery, cart, checkout and purchase. The project identifies the highest-friction stages and segments that deserve product attention.

## Objectives
1. Measure the conversion funnel.
2. Identify stage-level drop-offs.
3. Compare conversion by device, traffic source, category and price segment.
4. Quantify cart abandonment.
5. Translate findings into product hypotheses and recommendations.
6. Define KPIs and an A/B testing approach.

## Funnel
**Product View → Add to Cart → Checkout → Purchase**

## Product Opportunities
- Mobile checkout optimization
- Cart abandonment recovery
- Product-page optimization
- Premium product trust improvements
- Traffic-source-specific landing pages

## KPIs
**Primary:** purchase conversion rate, checkout completion rate, cart abandonment rate, revenue per visitor.

**Guardrails:** average order value, refund/cancellation rate, customer complaints.

## Interview Story
**Data → Funnel → Segmentation → Insight → Hypothesis → Product Recommendation → KPI → A/B Test**

## Limitations
Synthetic data is used. Findings demonstrate the analysis methodology and decision-making process rather than actual business results.
